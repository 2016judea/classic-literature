Classic Literature

Privacy

Last updated November 22, 2026. Classic Literature is run by Brick and Mortar AI LLC, Saint Paul, Minnesota, which is responsible for the connector and this site. It needs nothing about you: no account, no name, no email.

What the connector receives

Only what your assistant (Claude, ChatGPT or another app) sends with a tool call: a book title, an author's name, an era or a genre. It does not receive your conversation, your name, your email or your account with that app.

What it does with it

The title or name is matched against the corpus and then dropped when the call ends. It is not stored, not logged, not used to train any model, and not sold or shared. No model runs on our side when the connector answers.

What is counted

For each call we record one event in PostHog: which tool ran, whether it worked, how long it took, and the name of the app that called it (such as Claude or ChatGPT). That event carries nothing that identifies you or your conversation: no IP address, no account, and none of your words. These events are kept while the connector runs, to see which tools are used and which fail.

This website

We count visits with Vercel Web Analytics (no cookies) and PostHog: the pages viewed, and whether a visit came from the connector (links it hands out carry src=mcp). Links carry the page and the book or author you were looking at, never anything about you.

If you type a question into the website's question box, it is sent to Anthropic's API, together with a summary of the site's own data, to choose which chart to show. The question is not stored by us. Each IP address is limited to a few questions every ten minutes; that count is held in memory and not written anywhere.

Who else handles it

Vercel hosts the connector and this site and keeps standard request logs (time, path, status) under its own privacy policy. The request body, which holds your question, is not written to those logs. PostHog stores the counts above. Anthropic receives a website question as described. Nothing is sold.

The data

Measurements of public-domain texts from Project Gutenberg and published research. Cormac McCarthy's novels are in copyright and are not redistributed; only derived measurements appear.

Your choices

You can disconnect the connector in your app's settings at any time. Because nothing kept here identifies you, there is no personal data held to access, export or delete; write to us if you want that confirmed.

Children

The connector and site are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly receive their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Questions or privacy requests: brickandmortar.dev/contact.

Every number comes from measurements of the books' full texts, published with the code at github.com/2016judea/classic-literature.
