Classic Literature

Ask Claude about classic novels, from their full texts

Add Classic Literature as a connector and Claude can read a novel's emotional arc, find the writers who sound most alike, trace who influenced whom, and say what genre a book's own prose puts it in. Every number comes from the data, not from the model.

Server URL

https://classic-literature-bi.vercel.app/api/mcp

Streamable HTTP. No account, no key, nothing to sign in to. Thirteen read-only tools.

Add it to Claude

On claude.ai or the Claude app: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector. Name it Classic Literature, paste the URL above, and save. Then turn it on in a chat from the tools menu.

In Claude Code:

claude mcp add --transport http classic-literature https://classic-literature-bi.vercel.app/api/mcp

The tools

  • novel_mood_arc: a novel's emotional arc across 100 parts of its full text, its shape, where it turns, and the five novels whose arcs move most alike. 339 novels, 1678 to 1928.
  • writers_who_sound_like: the nearest writers by vocabulary and by ideas, out of 77 from Homer to Joyce, plus what a small GPT trained on McCarthy, Melville or Shakespeare makes of the others.
  • influence_chain: the shortest chain of documented influence between two authors, link by link, with its sources.
  • author_influences: who influenced an author and whom they influenced.
  • genre_of_book: the genre a novel's prose places it in, recovered from the words alone, or the genres of all of an author's novels.
  • genre_history: when a genre formed in the prose, and when its name caught on in print.
  • books_like: novels like a given one, by prose genre and by emotional arc.
  • does_book_have_a_happy_ending: whether a novel ends happier or sadder than it begins, in one line, then the full arc.
  • saddest_and_happiest_novels: the corpus ranked by overall mood, the mood of the ending, or how far it moves.
  • novels_by_author: an author's novels in the corpus, each with its prose genre.
  • classic_novels_from_era: the novels from a year range or a genre.
  • did_author_influence: a yes or no on one author influencing another, and the chain if it runs through others.
  • what_genres_are_there: every genre recovered from the prose and every genre-name clock.

Then ask

  • What's the emotional arc of Moby-Dick, and which novels move most like it?
  • Who writes most like Herman Melville, by vocabulary and by ideas?
  • How is Plato connected to Virginia Woolf through literary influence?

The corpus is public-domain novels up to 1928. Ask about a book outside it and Claude says so and offers the closest titles. Each answer links to the same view in the workspace. Privacy.

Every number comes from measurements of the books' full texts, published with the code at github.com/2016judea/classic-literature.
